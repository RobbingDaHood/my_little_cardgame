# Vision & Roadmap Suggestions (from B2.6 Woodcutting Balance Tuning)

These suggestions are based on learnings from tuning woodcutting balance
to meet the tier-based yield/durability targets from issue #66.

## vision.md suggestions

1. **Document card depletion as a design feature** — During woodcutting
   balance tuning, a key discovery was that player cards deplete over
   encounters (~1 card lost per encounter because the final play in a
   max_plays encounter has no draw). After ~5 encounters, the hand empties
   and subsequent encounters conclude immediately with 0 yield and 0
   durability cost. This is not a bug — it's the natural consequence of
   "cards are never reset between encounters." Vision.md should document
   this depletion dynamic as an intended session arc: early encounters are
   resource-rich, later encounters are barren unless the player manages
   card economy. This applies to ALL gathering disciplines.

2. **max_plays as a primary balance lever** — Reducing woodcutting max_plays
   from 8 to 4 was the single most effective change for creating strategic
   differentiation. With fewer plays, random luck produces weaker patterns
   while deliberate pattern-building still achieves strong multipliers.
   Vision.md should recommend max_plays as a first-order tuning parameter
   for pattern-based disciplines: lower max_plays = more strategy impact,
   higher max_plays = more RNG convergence.

3. **Between-encounter card replenishment** — Currently no gathering
   discipline has a mechanism to replenish hand cards between encounters.
   The session is bounded by hand depletion rather than durability budget.
   Vision.md should document whether this is the intended primary session
   limiter, or whether a "draw to max hand at encounter start" mechanic
   should be added. If hand depletion is intended, the balance targets
   should be calibrated knowing that only the first N encounters (where
   N ≈ starting hand size) are "active."

4. **Clarify yield/durability ratio semantics during depletion** — When
   most encounters contribute 0/0 (zero yield, zero durability), the
   yield/durability ratio is driven by a small number of early encounters.
   This means the ratio is very sensitive to early-game patterns. Vision.md
   should note that yield/durability is an efficiency metric of ACTIVE
   encounters, not a throughput metric of the full session.

## roadmap.md suggestions

1. **Card replenishment system** — Consider adding a card replenishment
   mechanism (e.g., "draw to max hand at start of each encounter" or
   "discard pile recycles to hand between encounters") as a future feature.
   This would make the full durability budget meaningful instead of just
   the first ~5 encounters. Currently, 10000 WoodcuttingDurability lasts
   far longer than the card supply, making durability irrelevant as a
   session limiter.

2. **Per-discipline max_plays tuning pass** — After woodcutting, consider
   reducing max_plays for other pattern/gathering disciplines if they show
   similar "RNG convergence" problems where random and strategic play
   produce indistinguishable results.

3. **Balance doc: add simulation results section** — The woodcutting_balance
   doc explicitly says simulation results belong in PRs, not the doc. But
   having a "Latest Tuned Values" table showing the most recent passing
   config would help future tuners establish baselines without re-running
   simulations. Consider adding a brief reference table.

## woodcutting_balance.md suggestions

1. **Update Strategy Hierarchy table** — Add DurabilityConserverStrategy
   as the second tier-2 runner. Current doc only lists PatternBuilder as
   tier-2.

2. **Document actual tuned config values** — The Config Parameters section
   lists categories but not current values. After tuning:
   - max_plays: 4 (reduced from 8)
   - Durability costs: 12–35 (absolute)
   - Stamina costs: 10–25 (absolute)
   - Health costs: 15–20 (absolute)
   - Base reward: 30 Lumber
   - Pattern multipliers boosted: Triple 3.0×, Four of Kind 5.0×,
     Full House 6.0×, Value Triple 7.0×, Value Quads 12.0×

3. **Add card depletion note** — Mention that with 5 starting hand cards
   and 1 card lost per encounter, the effective active encounter window
   is ~5 encounters per game. All yield/durability calibration assumes
   this depletion pattern.
